Turn your server's best moments into legends, and give the credit to the members who made them. Hall of Fame reposts your most reacted messages to a hall of fame channel with their images, videos and reply context, then ranks the people who keep landing there.

🏆 A hall of fame, not an archive

Any emoji your community actually reacts with can immortalise a message, not just ⭐. The board is only half of it: every featured message is credited to its author, so the server gets leaderboards, member profiles with monthly and all time ranks, and a yearly Hall of Fame Wrapped. Getting featured is meant to be worth something.

🌟 What you get

🔹 Multi-Emoji Magic: Any emoji can trigger immortality, custom server emojis included.

🔹 Media Support: Images and videos travel with the post, still playable.

🔹 Context Preservation: Keeps replies visible for the full story.

🔹 Leaderboards and Profiles: See who gets featured most, this month and all time.

🔹 Hall of Fame Wrapped: A year in review for every member and for the server.

🔹 Sensible Default: The starting threshold is picked from your member count on join, then it is yours to set.

🚀 Quick Start Guide

1. Invite: Add the bot and accept the permissions it asks for up front, granting them later does not work.

2. Channel: It creates a #hall-of-fame channel for you, or point it at your own with /set_hall_of_fame_channel.

3. Relax: A starting threshold is picked from your member count so the board works out of the box. Change it whenever you like with /set_reaction_threshold.

⚙️ Advanced Configuration

🔸 /set_reaction_threshold — How many reactions a message needs. 🔸 /calculation_method — Count the top emoji, all reactions, or unique people. 🔸 /hide_hof_post_below_threshold — Hide posts again when reactions are removed. 🔸 /require_image_or_video — Only feature messages with media. 🔸 /leaderboard — Rank the members who get featured most. 🔸 /get_server_config — See everything at a glance.

🔒 Emoji Whitelisting

Control exactly which reactions count toward the Hall of Fame:

🔸 /custom_emoji_check_logic — Toggle whitelist mode. 🔸 /whitelist_emoji — Add approved reactions. 🔸 /unwhitelist_emoji — Remove reactions.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Does it only work with the star emoji?

No. Every emoji counts by default, custom server emojis included. Narrow it down any time with the whitelist.

How many reactions does a message need?

Whatever you set with /set_reaction_threshold. When the bot joins it picks a starting value from your member count, once, so that the board works before you configure anything. It stays on that value until you change it.

Does it repost images and videos?

Yes. Images, videos and stickers travel with the post, and videos stay playable instead of becoming a bare link.

What happens when someone removes a reaction?

The count updates live, and a post that falls back under the threshold is hidden until it climbs over again.

Can I see who gets featured most?

Yes, that is the point of it. /leaderboard ranks the server and /user_profile shows a member's standing.

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source, with no paid tier and nothing locked behind voting.
