The recognition

Getting featured is worth something

Every post is credited to its author, so your server has something to compete over all year.

  • /leaderboard ranks members by posts and reactions earned, this month and all time.
  • /user_profile shows a member's ranks, best post, the gap to the next rank and their totals across servers.
  • Badges for monthly champions, 10 to 500 posts, months in a row and a 100-reaction post.
  • /hof_wrapped turns each member's year into a story with a shareable plaque, and /server_hof_wrapped does the same for the server.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does it only work with the star emoji?

No. Every emoji counts by default, custom server emojis included. Narrow it down any time with the whitelist.

Nothing is being posted. What is wrong?

Run /troubleshoot. The usual cause is channels behind a role the bot does not have, and it names that role.

What happens when someone removes a reaction?

The count updates live, and a post that falls under the threshold hides until it climbs back. /hide_hof_post_below_threshold turns that off.

Does it store our messages?

No. It keeps IDs, reaction counts and statistics, never message text.

Can I use it outside a server?

Yes. Add it to your account from /invite to see your own profile and manage your data anywhere.

Is it free?

Yes. No paid tier, and nothing locked behind voting.
