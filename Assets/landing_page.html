Turn your server's best moments into legends, and give the credit to the members who made them. Hall of Fame reposts your most reacted messages to a hall of fame channel with their images, videos and reply context, then ranks the people who keep landing there. Free, and running in 600+ servers.

🏆 A hall of fame, not an archive

Any emoji your community actually reacts with can immortalise a message, not just ⭐. The board is only half of it: every featured message is credited to its author, so the server gets leaderboards, member profiles with badges, and a yearly Hall of Fame Wrapped. Getting featured is meant to be worth something.

✨ New in 2.2
  • Profiles with badges. /user_profile shows this month, all time and every server side by side, the member's best post, the gap to the next rank, and badges such as monthly champion, months in a row or a 100-reaction post.
  • Totals across servers. Members featured in several servers see their posts added up, without any server being named.
  • Setup health check. /troubleshoot debugs your setup: it finds what stops a board from posting, from missing permissions to channels the bot cannot read, and names the role that would fix it.
  • Members control their data. /my_data shows, downloads or deletes everything stored about a member.

🌟 What you get

🔹 Every emoji counts: Any reaction can feature a message, custom server emojis included, or narrow it to a whitelist.

🔹 Three ways to count: The top emoji, all reactions together, or unique people.

🔹 Media support: Images and videos travel with the post, still playable.

🔹 Reply context: The message being replied to is included, so the punchline still lands.

🔹 Leaderboards and profiles: See who gets featured most, this month and all time, with badges for the members who keep landing there.

🔹 Hall of Fame Wrapped: A year in review for every member and for the server.

🔹 Works out of the box: The starting threshold is picked from your member count on join, and /troubleshoot tells you if anything is in the way.

🤔 Looking for a starboard?

People often arrive here searching for one, so here is the honest difference. A starboard archives messages that collected enough ⭐. Hall of Fame is about recognition, and the board is how it shows.

If all you want is a star archive, a starboard bot will do. If you want your server to compete over who gets immortalised, that is what this is for.

🔐 What it keeps about your members

  • No message text. The bot stores IDs, reaction counts and statistics. The repost lives in your own Hall of Fame channel on Discord, not in the bot's database.
  • Members are in control. Anyone can see, download or delete what is stored about them with /my_data, or ask not to be featured at all.
  • Removing the bot removes your server's data. Settings, featured posts and statistics are deleted when the bot leaves.
  • Written down. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is kept, and the source code is public.

🚀 Quick Start Guide

1. Invite: Add the bot and accept the permissions it asks for up front, granting them later does not work.

2. Channel: It creates a #hall-of-fame channel for you, or point it at your own with /set_hall_of_fame_channel.

3. Relax: A starting threshold is picked from your member count so the board works out of the box. Change it whenever you like with /set_reaction_threshold.

4. Nothing posting? Run /troubleshoot. If most of your channels are behind a role, it names the role the bot needs.

⚙️ Configuration

These need the Manage Server permission.

🔸 /set_reaction_threshold — How many reactions a message needs. 🔸 /set_hall_of_fame_channel — The channel the board posts to. 🔸 /calculation_method — Count the top emoji, all reactions, or unique people. 🔸 /include_authors_reaction — Whether reacting to your own message counts. 🔸 /hide_hof_post_below_threshold — Hide posts again when reactions are removed. 🔸 /require_image_or_video — Only feature messages with media. 🔸 /set_post_due_date — How old a message can be and still qualify. 🔸 /ignore_bot_messages — Whether other bots' messages can be featured. 🔸 /allow_messages_in_hof_channel — Let members chat in the board channel. 🔸 /count_in_global_profiles — Whether this server counts toward members' totals across servers. 🔸 /troubleshoot — Health check: find anything that stops messages reaching the board. 🔸 /get_server_config — See every setting at a glance.

🔒 Emoji Whitelisting

Control exactly which reactions count toward the Hall of Fame:

🔸 /custom_emoji_check_logic — Toggle whitelist mode. 🔸 /whitelist_emoji — Add approved reactions, up to 100. 🔸 /unwhitelist_emoji — Remove a reaction. 🔸 /clear_whitelist — Empty the whitelist.

🏅 For every member

🔸 /leaderboard — Rank the members who get featured most. 🔸 /user_profile — A member's ranks, best post, badges and totals across servers. 🔸 /hof_wrapped and /server_hof_wrapped — The year in review, for you or the whole server. 🔸 /my_data — See, download or delete what is stored about you. 🔸 /help, /invite, /vote, /feedback — Help, links and a way to reach the developer.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Does it only work with the star emoji?

No. Every emoji counts by default, custom server emojis included. Narrow it down any time with the whitelist.

How many reactions does a message need?

Whatever you set with /set_reaction_threshold. When the bot joins it picks a starting value from your member count, once, so that the board works before you configure anything. It stays on that value until you change it.

Nothing is being posted. What is wrong?

Run /troubleshoot, a health check for debugging the board. It checks the board's permissions, which channels the bot can read, the emoji whitelist and the threshold, and tells you what to change. The most common cause is channels hidden behind a role the bot does not have.

Does it repost images and videos?

Yes. Images, videos and stickers travel with the post, and videos stay playable instead of becoming a bare link.

What happens when someone removes a reaction?

The count updates live. By default a post that falls back under the threshold is hidden until it climbs over again, and /hide_hof_post_below_threshold turns that off so featured posts stay up.

Can I see who gets featured most?

Yes, that is the point of it. /leaderboard ranks the server and /user_profile shows a member's standing and badges.

Can I use it outside a server?

Yes. Add it to your account with the button under /invite, and you can look up your own profile and manage your data from DMs or any server.

Does it store our messages?

No. It keeps IDs, reaction counts and statistics, never message text. Members can see or delete their data with /my_data, and removing the bot deletes the server's data.

Is it free?

Yes, free with public source code, no paid tier and nothing locked behind voting.
