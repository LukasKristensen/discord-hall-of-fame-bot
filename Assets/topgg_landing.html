Hall of Fame 🏆✨

Capture legendary moments in your Discord server, and credit the members who made them.

Hall of Fame automatically tracks your most reacted messages and immortalises them on a hall of fame board once they pass a reaction threshold. Any emoji counts, images and videos travel with the post, and the members who keep getting featured earn leaderboards, profiles and a yearly wrapped for it.

🌟 Features

  • ✅ Every emoji counts – Not locked to a single reaction, custom server emojis included.
  • ✅ Images and videos – Media is reposted with the message, not linked.
  • ✅ Reply context – The message being replied to is included, so punchlines still land.
  • ✅ Leaderboards and profiles – See who gets featured most, this month and all time.
  • ✅ Hall of Fame Wrapped – A year in review for every member and for the server.
  • ✅ Custom reaction thresholds – Choose how many reactions a message needs.
  • ✅ Free and open source – No paid tier, no features held back behind voting.

🤔 Looking for a starboard?

A starboard archives messages that collected enough ⭐. Hall of Fame is about recognition: any emoji can immortalise a message, and the members behind them get ranked for it. If a plain star archive is all you need, a starboard bot will do the job.

🚀 Getting started

Invite the bot, accept the permissions it asks for, and it creates a #hall-of-fame channel. Point it at an existing channel instead with /set_hall_of_fame_channel.

So the board works straight away, the starting reaction threshold is picked once from your member count when the bot joins. Set it to whatever suits your server with /set_reaction_threshold, and it stays there.

🔒 Custom Emoji Whitelist

All emojis count by default. To narrow the board down to a specific set of reactions, or to a single custom server emoji, switch on the whitelist:

  • To enable it: /custom_emoji_check_logic
  • Adding emojis to the whitelist: /whitelist_emoji
  • Removing emojis from the whitelist: /unwhitelist_emoji or /clear_whitelist

💬 Command List

  • /help – List of available commands and their descriptions.
  • /set_reaction_threshold <reaction_threshold:int> – Set the amount of reactions needed for a post to reach the Hall of Fame.
  • /set_hall_of_fame_channel <channel> – Choose the channel the board posts to.
  • /calculation_method – Count the top emoji, all reactions, or unique people.
  • /include_authors_reaction – Should the author of a message be included in the reaction count?
  • /hide_hof_post_below_threshold – Hide posts again when reactions are removed.
  • /require_image_or_video – Only feature messages that contain media.
  • /set_post_due_date – How many days back a message can be and still qualify.
  • /allow_messages_in_hof_channel – Allow anyone to type in the Hall of Fame channel.
  • /custom_emoji_check_logic <All emojis | Only whitelisted emojis> – Use only whitelisted emojis for the reaction count.
  • /whitelist_emoji <emoji> – Add a whitelisted emoji to the list (used with /custom_emoji_check_logic).
  • /unwhitelist_emoji <emoji> – Remove a whitelisted emoji from the list (used with /custom_emoji_check_logic).
  • /clear_whitelist – Clear the whitelist of emojis (used with /custom_emoji_check_logic).
  • /leaderboard – View the Hall of Fame leaderboard for the server.
  • /user_profile <user> – Get the Hall of Fame profile for a member.
  • /hof_wrapped – Your personal Hall of Fame Wrapped for the year.
  • /server_hof_wrapped – The Hall of Fame Wrapped for the whole server.
  • /get_server_config – Get the current bot configuration for the server.
